HOW TO PLAY

  • Flippers — tap the left or right half of the table. Both at once works.
  • Launch — hold LAUNCH to charge the plunger, release to fire.
  • Nudge — tap NUDGE to shove the table. Three shoves and you tilt.
  • Keyboard — ← → flippers, space plunger, shift nudge, P pause.

SHOTS

  • Jet bumpers — fast points, and they keep the ball alive.
  • C-A-D-E-T targets — clear all five to light the kickback.
  • F-U-E-L lanes — in the dome. Complete them to raise the bonus multiplier (up to 5x).
  • Hyperspace — 50,000, then it spits the ball straight into the jet bumpers.
  • Black hole — 35,000, and it locks a ball. Lock three and you get MULTIBALL.
  • Orbit spinner — sits at the top of the right orbit. Loop the ball all the way round the dome to rip it.
